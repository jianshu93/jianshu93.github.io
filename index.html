Algorithms & microbial life

Biography

I am a computational microbiologist developing algorithms and open-source software for large-scale genomics, metagenomics, and microbiome analysis. I work at the intersection of probabilistic data structures, high-performance computing, and microbial ecology.

My research turns large biological datasets into tractable scientific questions. I develop methods for genome search, phylogenetic community comparisons, and biological network embedding, including DartUniFrac, GSearch, and Annembed. I also study microbial populations through long-read metagenomics and genome-resolved ecology.

I received my Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Georgia Tech in 2024, following an M.S. at Tsinghua University. My postdoctoral research at UC San Diego (2024–2026) focused on scalable methods for microbiome and genomic analysis. My primary coding language is Rust.
